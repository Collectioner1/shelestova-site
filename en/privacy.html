Policy on the processing of personal data

This Policy sets out the procedure for processing personal data and the measures to ensure its security applied by the operator.

1. General provisions

This Policy on the processing of personal data (hereinafter — the Policy) is drawn up in accordance with the requirements of Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27.07.2006 "On Personal Data" and sets out the procedure for processing personal data and the measures to ensure the security of personal data taken by the Operator.

The Operator's most important goal and condition for carrying out its activities is to respect the rights and freedoms of individuals when processing their personal data, including protection of the rights to privacy, personal and family secrecy.

2. Key concepts

Personal data — any information relating to a directly or indirectly identified or identifiable natural person (data subject).

Processing of personal data — any action (operation) or set of actions performed with or without the use of automation tools on personal data, including collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, clarification, use, transfer, blocking, deletion, and destruction.

3. Categories of data processed

The Operator processes the following personal data provided by the data subject through the forms on the website:

  • name;
  • phone number and/or messenger username (Telegram);
  • email address;
  • other information the data subject voluntarily provides in the text of their enquiry.

The Operator does not process special or biometric categories of personal data.

4. Purposes of processing

  • contacting the data subject to respond to their enquiry and agree the terms of services;
  • concluding and performing a contract for the provision of services;
  • informing about progress and sending reminders related to the provision of services.

5. Legal grounds

Processing is carried out on the basis of the data subject's consent, given by ticking a box in the form on the website, and also for the purpose of performing a contract to which the data subject is a party.

6. Procedure and conditions of processing

Personal data is processed with the consent of the data subject. The Operator takes the necessary legal, organisational, and technical measures to protect personal data from unlawful access, destruction, alteration, blocking, copying, and distribution.

Personal data is not transferred to third parties, except in the cases provided for by the legislation of the Russian Federation.

7. Terms of processing and storage

Personal data is processed until the purposes of processing are achieved or until the data subject withdraws consent. After the purposes are achieved or consent is withdrawn, the data is subject to deletion or anonymisation within a period not exceeding 30 days, unless otherwise provided by law.

8. Rights of the data subject

The data subject has the right to:

  • obtain information about the processing of their personal data;
  • demand clarification, blocking, or destruction of data if it is incomplete, outdated, inaccurate, or unlawfully obtained;
  • withdraw consent to the processing of personal data;
  • appeal the Operator's actions to the authorised body for the protection of the rights of data subjects (Roskomnadzor) or in court.

To exercise their rights, the data subject may send an enquiry to the e-mail: [email].

9. Withdrawal of consent

Consent to the processing of personal data may be withdrawn by the data subject at any time by sending a written notice to the Operator's e-mail. After withdrawal of consent, the Operator stops processing the data and deletes it within the periods established by law.

10. Cookies and analytics

The website may use cookies and web analytics systems (for example, Yandex.Metrica) to assess the convenience of the website. Certain technical data (IP address, browser data) may be processed in an anonymised form for statistical purposes.

11. Final provisions

The Operator may make changes to this Policy. The current version is always available on this page.
