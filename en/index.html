About me

Hi! My name is Elena

For more than 10 years I've been working with managers and teams, helping them develop management skills, improve work processes, and find solutions to complex organisational challenges.

My experience includes training and developing managers, coaching, facilitation, working with management teams, and process-improvement projects in large companies.

In my work I combine two approaches: working with people and a systemic view of how work is organised. This helps look at a problem not in isolation, but together with tasks, processes, and how people interact.

What matters to me is that after our work you're left not only with an understanding of the situation, but with concrete steps you can put into practice.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from training or coaching?
We work not with abstract theory but with your real situation. I combine a coaching approach (questions, insight) with a systemic view (processes, structure) — so the solution fits your specific case.
How does a session work?
Online, [60–90 minutes]. First we analyse the situation and what's blocking the result, then we find a solution and turn it into concrete steps.
What can I bring to a session?
Any work challenge: priorities and overload, delegation, difficult conversations with employees, building processes, distributing responsibility in a team.
How many sessions will I need?
One is often enough for a focused challenge. Sustainable change usually needs a bundle of several sessions.
What if it's not a good fit?
The first intro session (20 minutes) is free. If you feel it's not about your challenge, you pay nothing.
Is everything confidential?
Yes. Everything discussed in sessions stays between us.
Do you work with companies?
Yes, I work with management teams, run strategy sessions, and train managers. The format is agreed on request.
